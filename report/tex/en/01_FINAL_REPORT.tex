\section{2. Training Progression \& Evolution (\texttt{v1 Baseline $\rightarrow$ v2 Improved $\rightarrow$ v3 BEV Fixed})}

\subsection{2.1 Training Setup \& Architecture Evolution}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.8em,itemsep=2pt]
\item \textbf{\texttt{v1} Baseline (\texttt{YOLOv8s @ 640px})}: \texttt{epochs=30}, \texttt{batch=16}, \texttt{129 layers /\allowbreak{} 11.\allowbreak{}14M params /\allowbreak{} 28.\allowbreak{}6 GFLOPs}.
\item \textbf{\texttt{v2} Improved (\texttt{YOLO11m @ 960px} + Rare-Class Oversampling + \texttt{MobileSAM})}:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em,itemsep=2pt]
\item Upgraded input resolution from \texttt{640 $\rightarrow$ 960} with \texttt{C3k2 + C2PSA} spatial-attention \texttt{YOLO11m} (\texttt{231 layers /\allowbreak{} 20.\allowbreak{}06M params /\allowbreak{} 68.\allowbreak{}3 GFLOPs}).
\item Applied rare-class oversampling (\texttt{6,\allowbreak{}974} weighted train entries; Val/Test untouched), \texttt{cos\_\allowbreak{}lr=True}, \texttt{mixup=0.\allowbreak{}1}, \texttt{close\_\allowbreak{}mosaic=10}, \texttt{patience=12} on \textbf{\texttt{2$\times$ Tesla T4}} DDP (early-stopped at \texttt{Epoch 24}, Best = \textbf{\texttt{Epoch 12}}, \textasciitilde{}108 min).
\item Coupled \texttt{mobile\_\allowbreak{}sam.\allowbreak{}pt} (\texttt{38.\allowbreak{}8 MB}) Box-Prompt segmentation for pixel masks and ground-contact points (\texttt{mask\_\allowbreak{}bottom\_\allowbreak{}xy}).
\end{itemize}
\item \textbf{\texttt{v3} BEV Visual \& False-Alarm Fix (\texttt{world\_\allowbreak{}model.\allowbreak{}py}, \texttt{world\_\allowbreak{}visualizer.\allowbreak{}py})}:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em,itemsep=2pt]
\item Fixed >2x lateral distortion of longitudinal uncertainty ellipses in the BEV panel via Jacobian projection $J=\text{diag}(s_x,-s_y)$, lateral covariance decoupling, approach-speed gating (\texttt{approach\_\allowbreak{}w}), and per-track EMA smoothing.
\end{itemize}
\end{enumerate}

\section{7. Conclusions, Technical Challenges, and Reflections}

Key engineering lessons, debugging experiences, and limitations encountered while iterating from \texttt{v1 $\rightarrow$ v2 $\rightarrow$ v3 $\rightarrow$ v4}:


\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.8em,itemsep=2pt]
\item \textbf{Guarding Against Temporal Data Leakage via Sequence-Disjoint Splits}:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em,itemsep=2pt]
\item Random frame-level splits inflate \texttt{mAP} on \texttt{10 Hz} driving video because adjacent frames are nearly identical. Enforcing a strict \texttt{14 /\allowbreak{} 4 /\allowbreak{} 3} sequence-disjoint split---and excluding \texttt{595} MD5/dHash-matched frames when merging \texttt{kitti-\allowbreak{}stereo-\allowbreak{}image} (\texttt{7,\allowbreak{}481} images)---kept Held-out Test (\texttt{0013,\allowbreak{} 0016,\allowbreak{} 0019}) evaluation scientifically valid.
\end{itemize}
\end{enumerate}

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.8em,itemsep=2pt]
\item \textbf{Debugging the \texttt{testing/\allowbreak{}image\_\allowbreak{}02} vs \texttt{training/\allowbreak{}image\_\allowbreak{}02} Path Mismatch in \texttt{v4}}:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em,itemsep=2pt]
\item Visual inspection of early \texttt{v4} outputs revealed misaligned road/lane masks against the camera frames. Root-cause analysis showed \texttt{rglob('image\_\allowbreak{}02')} matched \texttt{testing/\allowbreak{}image\_\allowbreak{}02} (\texttt{0015}: \texttt{701}f, \texttt{0019}: \texttt{404}f) instead of \texttt{training/\allowbreak{}image\_\allowbreak{}02} (\texttt{0015}: \texttt{376}f, \texttt{0019}: \texttt{1,\allowbreak{}059}f). Adding an explicit \texttt{'training' in path.\allowbreak{}parts} guard and re-running fixed the 1:1 alignment, underscoring that visual sanity checks are indispensable alongside scalar metrics.
\end{itemize}
\end{enumerate}

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.8em,itemsep=2pt]
\item \textbf{Mitigating \texttt{Cyclist} vs \texttt{Pedestrian} Confusion and \texttt{2$\times$ T4} DDP Tuning}:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em,itemsep=2pt]
\item The normalized confusion matrix revealed \texttt{0.\allowbreak{}26} of \texttt{Cyclist} ground truths were misclassified as \texttt{Pedestrian}. Combining \texttt{Van}/\texttt{Person\_\allowbreak{}sitting} \texttt{114}-gray masking, train-only rare-class oversampling, \texttt{1280px} scaling, \texttt{close\_\allowbreak{}mosaic=7}, and TTA raised Test Easy \texttt{mAP@0.\allowbreak{}5} to \texttt{0.\allowbreak{}8966} (\texttt{Cyclist Test Easy 0.\allowbreak{}7899}).
\end{itemize}
\end{enumerate}

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.8em,itemsep=2pt]
\item \textbf{Resolving BEV Uncertainty Distortion \& False-Alarm Spikes}:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em,itemsep=2pt]
\item Early \texttt{v2} BEV plots stretched longitudinal depth uncertainty laterally across adjacent lanes, triggering false \texttt{Emergency Intervention} alerts on sidewalk pedestrians. Jacobian projection ($J=\text{diag}(s_x,-s_y)$), approach-speed gating (\texttt{approach\_\allowbreak{}w}), and \texttt{CollisionRiskSmoother} (\texttt{1.\allowbreak{}10/\allowbreak{}s} slew-rate clamp + \texttt{0.\allowbreak{}04} hysteresis) eliminated \texttt{>1.\allowbreak{}5/\allowbreak{}s} spikes by \texttt{100\%} and cut \texttt{0015} false-alarm risk by \texttt{-\allowbreak{}57.\allowbreak{}0\%}.
\end{itemize}
\end{enumerate}

\item Monocular flat-ground projection (\texttt{h\_\allowbreak{}cam = 1.\allowbreak{}65m}) remains sensitive to road pitch variations at long range, and KITTI Tracking lacks closed-loop vehicle dynamics, restricting Safety Shield validation to offline advisory evaluation. Future work will fuse stereo/LiDAR depth and validate closed-loop control inside CARLA.
